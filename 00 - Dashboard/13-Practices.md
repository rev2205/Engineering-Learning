# 13 Engineering Practices

1. **Study real systems:** Read an engineering blog and draw its architecture from memory.
2. **Build vocabulary:** Learn 3–5 terms weekly with a definition, example and related term.
3. **Build and break projects:** Create a tiny service, observe it, introduce one safe failure, recover it.
4. **Follow thoughtful engineers:** Use social media to discover ideas, then verify technical claims.
5. **Read classic papers:** Start with abstract, problem, diagrams, evaluation, limitations and conclusion.
6. **Study outage postmortems:** Identify trigger, contributing conditions, detection, impact and prevention.
7. **Inspect AI-generated work:** Check assumptions, read diffs, test failure paths and verify against docs.
8. **Focus on one layer:** Spend enough time on networking, Linux, containers, orchestration or observability to build a model.
9. **Join open source gradually:** Start with docs, reproducible bug reports, tests and small fixes.
10. **Keep an engineering notebook:** Record question, hypothesis, evidence, explanation and next experiment.
11. **Practice retrieval:** Explain yesterday's concept before looking at notes.
12. **Teach simply:** Explain why a component exists and what fails without it.
13. **Connect ideas weekly:** Link concepts such as DNS + Kubernetes Services or TLS + certificates.

## Weekly scorecard
- [ ] Three short study sessions
- [ ] One practical experiment
- [ ] One concept explained from memory
- [ ] One article or incident analyzed
- [ ] One notebook entry
- [ ] One review and next-week goal
