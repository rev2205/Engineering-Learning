# Engineers and Publications to Follow

Use social media as discovery, not as the final authority. Verify claims with docs, code, papers or postmortems.

## People to explore
- Julia Evans (@b0rk): Linux, networking and debugging explanations.
- Charity Majors (@mipsytipsy): observability and production engineering.
- Brendan Gregg: systems performance and Linux profiling.
- Kelsey Hightower: Kubernetes and cloud-native systems.
- Liz Fong-Jones: observability and reliability.
- Gergely Orosz: engineering organizations and practice.
- Mitchell Hashimoto: developer tools and infrastructure.
- Jeff Geerling: automation and open-source projects.

## Publications
- [The Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/)
- [Google SRE](https://sre.google/)
- [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/)
- [Last Week in AWS](https://www.lastweekinaws.com/) — commentary; verify claims against primary sources.

Keep a short reading queue. Finish one long-form item before saving another. Popularity is not proof of correctness.
